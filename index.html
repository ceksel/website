About

I study how humans represent, remember, and navigate space across real and virtual environments. My work bridges spatial cognitionagingVRhuman‑centered design with mixed‑methods: behavioral tasks, immersive VR, and qualitative interviews.

  • Human‑centered spatial design
    Architectural affordances and environmental cues that improve wayfinding.
  • Methods
    Mixed‑methods (behavioral + qualitative), Virtual Silcton, SBSOD, task design.
